This project examines engagement and authenticity in interactive narratives using live action role-playing (LARP) games in China.

My contributions include constructing a dataset of player reviews, narrative scripts, and recorded gameplay, and developing OCR- and LLM-assisted pipelines to digitize and structure narrative materials for qualitative and computational analysis.
