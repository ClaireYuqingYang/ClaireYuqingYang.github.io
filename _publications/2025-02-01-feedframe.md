Accuracy+ examines goal-compatible accuracy nudges for reducing misinformation sharing in social media environments.

My contributions include building the experimental platform, implementing browsing-goal manipulations and behavioral trace logging, and co-developing the conceptual framework, pilot study, and preregistered analysis plan. I used mixed-effects and causal inference models to analyze behavioral data.

*Fang and Yang contributed equally.*
